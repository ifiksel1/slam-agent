The real mission shaping the SLAM stack choices (surfaced 2026-06-11 via a loop-closure research fork): **fully-autonomous drone inspection of a 737 inside a fixed hangar (~80-100m bounded volume), requiring cm-level DRONE POSE held *throughout* the flight.** This is the use case behind the EllipseLIO/SuperOdom/FAST-LIO work in [[new-slam-frameworks-2026-06]].

**Requirement clarified: cm-level POSE, not sub-cm surface measurement.** Critical distinction:
- Sub-mm SURFACE measurement (sizing dents/cracks/rivets) is NOT a SLAM job and NOT achievable from the Ouster OS1-64 (~1-3cm/pt range noise) — it needs a camera (photogrammetry) or structured-light scanner. LiDAR+IMU are only the POSE SCAFFOLD; the camera does the detail. If sub-cm ever means the measurement, it's a sensor problem, not a SLAM/loop-closure problem.
- cm-level POSE is achievable and is the actual target.

**The hard part is "throughout," and it's the degeneracy problem we already hit.** cm pose on AVERAGE is easy in a bounded volume (scan-to-map re-registers against structure that never leaves the active map). The threat is LOCALIZED DEGENERACY EXCURSIONS: facing a large smooth fuselage/wing or a bare wall at close standoff → geometry goes feature-poor → LiDAR constraint collapses in some axes → pose jumps tens of cm then recovers. **This is the SAME failure mode as the apartment bag's t≈84-90 feature-poor section — just more likely here** (aircraft skin + empty hangar are textbook degenerate surfaces). Open-loop LIO cannot GUARANTEE cm while staring at blank skin.

**DECIDED ARCHITECTURE (ranked by robustness):**
1. **Instrument the hangar (right answer — it's a fixed install).** Surveyed fiducials/AprilTags at known coords, OR a prior survey-grade map + real-time relocalization (**SuperOdom `localization_mode` does exactly this**). Why it wins for "throughout": a single visible anchor fixes pose EVEN during LiDAR degeneracy → bridges the blank-fuselage moments that break pure-LIO, gives bounded ABSOLUTE cm continuously, and is immune to the perceptual aliasing that makes appearance-based loop closure (BTC) dangerous in a symmetric hangar (repeating panels/trusses, L/R-symmetric aircraft).
2. **Un-instrumented path (user is leaning/committed to this per the 2026-06-12 research thread).** cm-throughout uninstrumented is MITIGATION, not guarantee — but reachable in a geometry-rich hangar via, in priority order:
   a. **A REAL eigenvalue-based degeneracy estimate** (NOT FAST-LIO's count — see FAST-LIO note below). Use EllipseLIO `obs_score` / SuperOdom per-axis uncertainty, or patch eigenvalues-of-HᵀH into FAST-LIO. This is the linchpin.
   b. **Perception-aware planning — the STRONGEST uninstrumented lever.** Because the drone is autonomous, the planner consumes the degeneracy estimate and AVOIDS degenerate viewpoints BY CONSTRUCTION: never fly nose-on into blank skin with nothing else in frame; always keep a second independent normal direction in FOV (hangar walls/floor/roof trusses, aircraft EDGES, the wing/fuselage intersection). A hangar is geometry-rich — degeneracy only happens if you let the LiDAR point at a single flat surface. Plan to retain geometric diversity and you hold the cm bound with no anchor.
   c. **Online BTC+PGO loop closure** to pin accumulated drift at the many revisits an orbit-the-aircraft mission generates.
   d. **IMU bridging** for brief unavoidable degenerate windows (holds cm ~1-2s; longer drifts).
   Residual risk: a LONG degenerate stretch with no good geometry and no loop in view WILL break the cm bound — unavoidable without an anchor.

**Loop closure is NOT the priority** — a useful negative result that avoids a big detour. Appearance-based BTC is only the un-instrumented SUBSTITUTE for an anchor; it buys global CONSISTENCY at revisits (stops fuselage doubling) but does NOT protect through blank-fuselage degenerate moments the way a fiducial does, and CONSISTENCY ≠ absolute cm. For a FIXED hangar, instrument once and turn "cm throughout" from a hope into a guarantee.

**Framework fit — degeneracy-awareness is now the deciding feature (not raw accuracy):**
- **EllipseLIO** — native `obs_score` degeneracy signal + adaptive ellipsoid residuals designed for confined/feature-poor spaces → natural FRONT-END, paired with anchors. (Front-end of choice; we tuned/validated it this session.)
- **SuperOdom** — explicit 6-DOF uncertainty + `localization_mode` (prior-map relocalization) → best when instrumented.
- **FAST-LIO** — no real degeneracy signal → weakest fit for a GUARANTEED cm bound. **CODE-VERIFIED (2026-06-12, FAST_LIO_GPU `laserMapping.cpp`):** the matched-plane normals ARE present (`h_share_model()` stores them in `corr_normvect`, line 757; each Jacobian `h_x` row's first 3 entries are that normal, line 799) — so the raw material for a Zhang-2016 eigenvalue degeneracy estimate is right there. BUT stock FAST-LIO only computes `effct_feat_num` (a COUNT of valid plane matches) and checks `< 1` ("No Effective Points!"); grep for `degener|eigen|observab|condition|singular|nullspace` finds NOTHING. **The count is the WRONG metric and actively LIES in our worst case: staring at a blank fuselage/wing gives thousands of effective points with near-PARALLEL normals → count is high, no warning, yet fully degenerate in the two in-plane translation axes.** Don't trust `effct_feat_num` as a degeneracy signal — it's high-and-happy exactly when most degenerate. A real signal needs eigenvalues of HᵀH (or the 3×3 normal covariance); that's a small well-defined patch (normals already in `h_x`) and what degeneracy-aware FAST-LIO forks add. EllipseLIO `obs_score` / SuperOdom uncertainty compute this directional version natively → why they beat FAST-LIO here as-shipped.

**⭐ THE worst-case viewpoint = "ABOVE THE AIRCRAFT" / nadir (code+geometry verified 2026-06-12).** Looking straight down, the dominant returns are wing tops, fuselage crown, and hangar floor — all large HORIZONTAL planes. A horizontal plane constrains only Z/roll/pitch and does NOT constrain X/Y/yaw → you go degenerate in horizontal-translation + heading, while ALTITUDE STAYS CORRECT and MASKS it. This is the count-trap made geometric: planar/surf features FLOOD (thousands, all vertical normals) → high count, full confidence, zero horizontal info; the edge/corner features that would pin X/Y/yaw are scarce on smooth skin. **Hidden variable: hangar wall distance** — your real horizontal constraint up there is whatever VERTICAL structure is in LiDAR range; a big hangar with a centered 737 puts walls 30-50m out → sparse/weak on a 64-beam OS1-64 → not enough to pin X/Y/yaw. **Planner rules to survive it uninstrumented:** never nadir-loiter low over flat skin; keep VERTICAL structure in FOV at all times — exploit the aircraft's own vertical features (the **vertical stabilizer/tail** is a huge vertical plane, plus engine nacelles, fuselage SIDES, landing gear, wing edges); fly higher/laterally-offset so peripheral walls stay in range; approach the top OBLIQUELY, never nadir-only; treat any above-pass as a SHORT timed IMU-bridged transit through degeneracy, re-anchoring on structure-rich geometry immediately before/after. **Also gate LOOP-CLOSURE ACCEPTANCE on the degeneracy signal** — ICP on a flat top slides freely in-plane and can return a confident-but-wrong transform that INJECTS error into the graph (the back-end is degenerate in the same X/Y/yaw axes as the front-end).

**LIO-SAM-GPU evaluated + rejected for this mission (2026-06-12).** The GPU port only accelerates the scan-to-map optimization (iVox-style hash KNN + line/plane Jacobian/residual math; ~85ms→21ms on Orin NX). It does NOT touch feature extraction, loop closure, or add degeneracy analysis → algorithmically STOCK LIO-SAM, just faster: edge+planar features, radius-search+ICP loop closure, and crucially NO observability readout. So it has the exact same nadir blind spot as FAST-LIO (floods planar features, no warning) PLUS a loop closure (radius+ICP) that's degenerate in the same axes. Reinforces EllipseLIO (`obs_score`) / SuperOdom (per-axis uncertainty) as the only two that would LIGHT UP above the aircraft instead of drifting silently. (If LIO-SAM-GPU were ever used, the fix is the same small patch: reduce JᵀJ, eigendecompose, flag small X/Y/yaw eigenvalues — the Jacobians are already computed.)

**Control-vs-map rule (applies to any global correction):** feed the SMOOTH LIO odometry to the flight controller (a loop-closure/relocalization correction is a global JUMP that destabilizes the position loop); let the planner/coverage logic work in the corrected global frame; build/refine the final map offline. Same kinematic-gate guidance as [[new-slam-frameworks-2026-06]].

**Loop-closure landscape (reference, from the research):** loop closure = place-recognition descriptor + GTSAM/iSAM2 PGO back-end. Descriptor families: handcrafted-global (Scan Context → SC++ → LiDAR-Iris → M2DP; CPU), handcrafted-triangle/HKU (STD → **BTC** [use over STD] → iBTC → DTD, BoW3D; CPU, viewpoint-invariant, best for reverse/large-viewpoint in structured scenes), learned (PointNetVLAD/MinkLoc3Dv2/OverlapTransformer/LCDNet; GPU+training). SC-PGO (Giseop Kim) is the odometry-agnostic plug-in (SC detector + GTSAM), already wired to FAST-LIO2 — NOT the same as SC-LIO-SAM (that's SC-PGO integrated into LIO-SAM; stock LIO-SAM uses radius+ICP, not SC). For a drone's varied-6DOF revisits, BTC out-recalls plain SC and stays CPU-only (matters on the no-spare-GPU Orin NX). Survey: *LiDAR-Based Place Recognition for Autonomous Driving* (ACM CSUR 2024).

**DIRECTION (2026-06-12): pursuing the UN-INSTRUMENTED path** (no surveyed fiducials/prior map). So the plan centers on: EllipseLIO front-end (native `obs_score`) + perception-aware planning that steers for geometric diversity + online BTC loop closure + IMU bridging — accepting mitigation-not-guarantee. The instrumented path (fiducials / SuperOdom `localization_mode`) stays documented as the more robust alternative if cm-throughout proves unreliable. Empirical pre-check before any backend work: record a representative hangar flight, fly a few closed orbits, inspect the map for fuselage/wall doubling — if it stays sharp, tuned LIO + degeneracy gating already suffices. Still useful to know: hangar dimensions (sets planning scale).
