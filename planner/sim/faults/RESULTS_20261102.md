### What changed

- **SLAM frame reset (3a, 3b), the bridge.** SUPER offers no map reset (its only input is the click goal; ROG-map clears
  only when the pose leaves its 30 m local map), so SUPER is not patched. The bridge carries the planner frame over the
  reset instead (bridge_core `FrameCarry`): a raw pose after a gap (> `raw_gap_s` 0.5 s) or a step (> `raw_jump_m` 0.5 m /
  `raw_jump_deg` 15) is held, nothing published, until its vision pose classifies it (`classify_frame_change`): the old
  anchor misses the vision pose by more than max(`frame_reset_min_m` 1.0, `frame_reset_rate` 0.25 x gap) or
  `frame_reset_min_deg` 5 → a new raw frame: `/planner/odom`, `/planner/cloud` and `/planner_bridge/anchor` go on in the
  planner frame they had, so SUPER's map and trajectory, the track planner's memory and the landing check's scan windows
  stay valid. Less → FAST-LIO resumed in its own frame (2a: the anchor change is the EKF's drift), published as before.
- **Re-anchor hold (3a, 3b), the bridge.** After any re-anchor in flight the HOLD lasts until SUPER's committed trajectory
  started after it and from the vehicle (`plan_after`, `reanchor_plan_start_tol` 0.5 m); a fresh command no longer ends it.
  After a SLAM-stale spell the hold waits `slam_settle_s` 0.5 s with SLAM fresh. Defence in depth (`clamp_vertical`): a
  flight target's z moves at most `flight_vz_max` (speed cap) per second, lies within `flight_z_lead` 0.5 m of the
  vehicle, its vertical feed-forward at most the speed cap. Status: `frame_resets`, `reanchor`, `slam_pending`,
  `planner_pos`, `z_clamped`, `planner_goal`, `planner_goal_clear`.
- **The executive's SLAM hold (3a, 10-28 note 5).** A re-anchor hold waits for a plan, and SUPER at rest plans only on a
  goal: with SLAM back the goal goes again after `map_rebuild_s` while the bridge still holds (re-seed after
  `planner_stuck_s`). A resent goal is checked first (mission.h `check_resent_goal`): the executive's anchor against the
  bridge's `planner_pos`, the goal at least `resend_floor_min` 0.3 m above the surface under the vehicle. The resent z of
  lf11 (-0.002) was correct for the new frame; the climb came from SUPER's old map. HOLD_SLAM over `hold_slam_timeout_s`
  20 s: QRLOITER `unreachable_hold_s`, then QRLAND (ground: the drive stop), STOPPED "SLAM hold timed out".
- **FC link (7c).** /mavros/state silent `fc_state_timeout_s` 1.5 s with the FC pose silent `fc_pose_link_timeout_s` 1.5 s,
  or the state alone `fc_state_max_s` 3 s: STOPPED "FC link lost", bridge off, no resume. 1.0 / 0.5 s gave a false loss
  in SITL (fx00, first build): the state comes with the 1 Hz heartbeat, measured gaps up to 2.06 s, FC pose up to 1.11 s.
- **Bridge heartbeat (1c, 1d, note 9).** Enabled by us, its status (5 Hz) and its setpoints both silent
  `bridge_status_timeout_s` 0.5 s: "planner bridge died". Driving: the executive streams the drive stop itself
  (MASK_STOP 1479 on /mavros/setpoint_raw/local) until at rest; flying: a hover target at the vehicle
  `bridge_dead_hover_s` 1 s, then QRLOITER; STOPPED.
- **Drive stop after a stop (2d).** Every STOPPED and ground UNREACHABLE / REFUSED in GUIDED streams the drive stop until
  the FC's speed stays under `drive_stop_rest_speed` 0.05 m/s for `drive_stop_rest_s` 1 s (at least `drive_stop_min_s`
  1 s, at most `drive_stop_timeout_s` 10 s; a frozen SLAM pose: the whole 10 s, the FC's speed follows the frozen pose).
- **Goal inside an obstacle (4a).** With the vehicle within `goal_end_near` 0.35 m of SUPER's plan's end, that end steady
  (`goal_end_steady` 0.1 m) and more than `goal_shift_max` 0.6 m above or below our goal for `goal_clear_s` 1 s: the leg
  ends "goal in an obstacle" (on_unreachable applies). Any fly leg given up in the air first backs off (BACK_OFF): up to
