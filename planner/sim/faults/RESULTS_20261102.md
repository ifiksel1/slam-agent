# L2 failure tests: re-run on the current L4 build (2026-11-02)

The suite of `RESULTS.md` (2026-10-28), run again case by case on HEAD 7da4bc5 (branch boot-services): the C++
robot nodes (l4_bridge, l4_executive, l4_track, l4_landing), the frozen-pose detection (75cb587), the SUPER
from-rest fix (dfb17db, image `l4-sim-l2:latest` = superfix), the drive stop and track planner changes, and
today's landing changes (7da4bc5). Same map (`hp_1015_carved.pcd`, start 0, 0, yaw -90.7), the same missions
and triggers, one case per run, SITL only.

## How (what differs from 2026-10-28)

- Shannon `~/sim/l2fault` as `L2_BASE`: `planner/` and `planner_sim/` from `git archive` of 7da4bc5 (plus the two
  script changes below), `slam/` = `~/sim/l2/slam`, the C++ install a copy of `~/sim/landsrch2/cpp_ws/install`
  (the build of 7da4bc5's planner/ros; its sources match the commit) for both `L4_WS` and `L4_BRIDGE_WS`.
  Runner `~/sim/l2fault/run_batch.sh` (`faults/batch.sh` with that env). Runs `~/sim/l2fault/runs/lf*`, bags kept.
- Containers pinned to cores 20-27 with 8 GiB each (`DOCKER_LIMITS`), and the batch waited while another agent's
  SITL ran (`WAIT_CMD`).
- Reruns for a SITL watchdog reset ("GOT SIGALRM"): lf15 → lf15r, lf07 → lf07r. Nothing else was rerun.
- `cases.sh` and `inject.py` already used the C++ node names (`^/ws[^ ]*/lib/l4_track/track_planner`,
  `.../l4_bridge/planner_bridge`, executive `/lib/l4_executive/mission_executive`). lf05 and lf06 confirm the kills hit.
- New in the list since 10-28: `hover_park` (no false frozen-pose trigger while hovering or parked).
- The landing legs now run the 7da4bc5 flow (LAND_CLIMB to 1.6 m over the surface below, check, search, LAND_MOVE
  through SUPER). 6 (land_occupied) is therefore judged against the search design: land elsewhere, clear of the crate.
- Distances are from `/sim/truth`, as before. "Along" is along the velocity at the fault. Box clearance is from the
  FC centre to the box surface; the frame reaches 0.25 m out (0.10 m below the centre).
- Tables: `faults/results.py runs/lf*` (`~/sim/l2fault/results_table.md`); timelines `faults/trace.py`.
  For the obstacle cases also `~/sim/l2fault/obst_trace.py` (SUPER / track commands against the box) and
  `pathbox.py` (the track planner's paths against the box).

## Script changes

- `planner/sim/sitl_harness.sh`: optional `DOCKER_LIMITS`, extra `docker run` args for both containers
  (e.g. `--cpuset-cpus 20-27 --memory 8g`). On a shared host, taskset on the caller does not reach the containers.
- `planner/sim/faults/batch.sh`: optional `WAIT_CMD`. Before each case it waits while the command prints something,
  e.g. another agent's SITL container.
- Not changed, cosmetic: batch.sh's "done" log line greps `"end": {...}` on one line, but `fault_result.json` is
  indented, so the line is empty. Use results.py.

## Notes

A. **A drive-mode HOLD streams nothing (2c, 3c, 5b).** `planner_bridge_node.cpp`, drive branch: "HOLD on the
   ground: Rover Guided stops at the last target". Only planner_dead and slam_frozen send the zero-speed velocity +
   heading stop (mask 2503). On a SLAM-stale hold, Rover Guided drives on to its last run-out target and then
   wanders. lf05 shows the zero-speed stop itself works (0.64 m). Fix: use it for every drive HOLD (SLAM stale,
   NO_PATH, planner stale, REACHED). This is in the obstfix work.
B. **The executive's STOPPED cancels the bridge's drive stop (2d).** On slam_frozen in drive the bridge sent the
   zero-speed stop at +1.0 s, the executive went STOPPED at +1.02 and disabled the bridge at +1.2 s, and the stop was
   gone (lf10 setpoints: one 2503 target, then nothing). The rover coasted 3.6 m. Fix (small): in drive mode, keep the
   bridge enabled with the stop latched after STOPPED (or have the bridge keep streaming a latched stop after a
   disable while on the ground), until the rover is at rest. With the EKF fed the frozen pose, whether the FC's own
   speed estimate lets a velocity target brake is a further question: the proposed SLAM-to-FC bridge change (cut
   vision on a frozen pose, `overrides/FAST-LIO/proposed/`) would make the FC's estimate honest.
C. **SLAM reset handling (D7) in flight is still broken, now unsafely (3a, 3b).**
   - 3b: no hold at all. No HOLD_SLAM was reported, and the bridge latched nothing visible in 5 Hz status. The anchor
     changed under SUPER's map and plan, and the targets it produced in the new frame climbed 2.2 m and ran 4.8 m past
     the goal. The 10-28 note 5 analysis stands: SUPER's ROG-map and committed trajectory are still in the old planner
     frame.
   - 3a: the deadlock is gone (the "fresh command" now comes from SUPER on SLAM return), but that command is planned in
     the old frame: surge 1.45 m/s and climb to 2.38 m within 3 s. The executive's resent goal had `goal_planner` z
     -0.002 (fc z 1.05 converted with the new anchor) and was never reached.
   - Fix (as 10-28 note 5, now urgent): on a re-anchor, reset SUPER (clear its map and committed trajectory; a goal at
     the vehicle or a restart) and hold until a command planned after the re-anchor. In the bridge, clamp the target's
     z step and lead after a re-anchor. In the executive, check the resent goal's planner z against the vehicle's.
D. **4a: SUPER cannot plan from over the box (from-rest failures).** It flew the goal SUPER shifted to the box's top
   (0.15 m above it); from there every PlanFromRest failed (backup "GeneratePolytopeFromLine failed",
   exp opt failed), ~100 failures/s for 40 s (the SUPER leak agent's repro case). The executive re-seeded twice per
   leg, gave up, then stopped on the next leg too. 10-28 got out of it. Fix: the executive's give-up on a goal inside
   an obstacle should climb or back off to known-free space (or land) before the next leg; SUPER's goal shift
   (`getNearestInfCellNot`, 3 m) should not put the goal within robot_r of an obstacle top.
E. **5a / 5b mechanisms (for the obstfix and SUPER agents):**
   - 5a (10-28, f25r): SUPER committed a trajectory at +0.11 s, and the pillar was in /planner/cloud at +0.12 s. Every
     replan from +0.2 to +4.7 s failed (backup "BackOpt Pos violation", from +2.8 s exp opt). SUPER kept executing the
     +0.11 s committed trajectory, which ran through the pillar (no committed_traj publish between +0.11 and +4.77 s;
     the bridge streamed it, all STREAM). SUPER has no check of the committed trajectory against the map when a replan
     fails, and fsm.cpp's EMER_STOP is unreachable (generateExpTraj never returns EMER). ROG-map had the pillar in time
     (CIRI reported it at 0.15 m), so the raycast, decay and inflation settings are not the cause.
   - 5a (lf16): replans succeeded and SUPER went round. The contact came from the bridge's waypoint hold using the
     executive's raw goal (0.19 m from the pillar) instead of SUPER's shifted goal.
   - 5b (lf17): the box was seen in 0.3-0.4 s (10-28: 8.9 s). The contact came from note A (no stop on NO_PATH) at SITL's
     over-driven 1.1 m/s (WP_SPEED 0.5).
   - A secondary risk in l4_track's evidence memory (`track_core.cpp` LiveWindow::correct): an occupied cell is cleared as
     "moved" while its decayed see-through count exceeds 1.5x its hits (tau 10 s). An obstacle that appears where the
     sensor had looked through for seconds can therefore read free for seconds. That likely explains the 10-28 8.9 s.
F. **7c: QRLAND without vision is not a safe landing.** FS_EKF now goes to QRLAND (10-28: QRALTHOLD, note 2), but
   with no position source the descent drifted at 2.0-2.5 m/s horizontally. After touch-down QuadRover switched to
   MANUAL and the SITL rover rolled at a constant 0.64 m/s with no command for 20 s (lf08 similarly at 0.11 m/s). The
   roll is likely SITL's ground model (no braking at zero throttle in MANUAL), not the robot; check on the bench.
G. **2b: QRLAND on a frozen pose is not safe either.** The vision feed keeps the frozen pose, the EKF keeps fusing
   it, and QRLAND's position hold flies the vehicle off while descending (2.2 m/s, 1.39 m/s ground strike). Options
   for the owner: the SLAM-to-FC bridge cutting vision on a frozen pose while airborne (proposed, not applied), so the
   EKF failsafe or a pure-altitude descent takes over; or ~slam_frozen_flight_mode QRALTHOLD-descend (a param
   decision, not changed here).
8. (10-28 note 8) **The executive survives MAVROS dying now** (lf21: the QRLOITER call failed and was logged, no
   crash) **but still does not see the loss**: it ended on UNREACHABLE at +19.9 s (lf21) and +18.6 s (lf22), not on
   "FC link lost". A stale /mavros/state should end the leg at once.
9. (10-28 note 9) The executive still does not notice a dead bridge (1c, 1d: 21-24 s to UNREACHABLE). A bridge
   status heartbeat check would end it sooner.
6. (10-28 note 6) unchanged: an 8 s SLAM outage shifts the fc-frame mission ~1.2 m in the world (2a, 2c). In lf09 the
   final land check then refused a "not flat" spot and the mission STOPPED in QRLOITER.
11. (10-28 note 11) fc_pose_stale flaps are much rarer (baseline: 1 in 131 s vs 18 in 93 s; hover_park 16 in 202 s).
12. (10-28 note 12) The 1.0 m/s cap is still exceeded in fault transients: 1.45 m/s (3a, 3b). Driving, SITL's rover
    runs 1.1-1.15 m/s at WP_SPEED 0.5 (its motor model; closed_loop.sh header). That makes the drive obstacle cases
    harder than on the robot at 0.5 m/s, but the robot's own cap is 1.0 m/s.
